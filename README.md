# SMASHROOM

A rage room in your browser. Two minutes, one room, everything breaks.

**Play:** https://wasiulbahar.com/smashroom/

## What's in it

- **Two looks.** *Stylized* (bright cartoon, runs on almost anything) or *Realistic* (real textures and models, see-through glass, soft shadows, dust and glow; needs more PC power, quality drops automatically if it lags).
- **Twenty rooms, plus an open city.** Classic rage room, abandoned building, haunted house, corporate office, kitchen, apartment, car yard (smash whole cars), a clay studio and a Downtown city street, plus two calm rooms: a sunny greenhouse and a beach shack at sunset.
- **Clay figure.** A life-size clay statue of a human body that you punch, slap and kick with bare hands. Every hit leaves a dent, slaps turn its head, it sways and groans, arms, legs and head break off at the joints, and every third figure is a tougher gold boss.
- **Four modes.** Classic (2 minutes), Zen (no timer, calm music), Time attack (clear the room as fast as you can) and Target practice (throw things at targets on the walls).
- **Things that react.** Gas cans explode and set off chain reactions, golden items are worth 10× for a few seconds, and cup pyramids topple.
- **Game feel.** Hit-stop on big hits, camera kick, on-screen combo calls and phone vibration.
- **Best smash replay.** Your best moment plays back in slow motion at the end, and you can save it as a video clip.
- **Smash, grab and throw.** 40+ breakable things: glass, china, TVs, monitors, printers, laptops, furniture, cinder blocks. Thrown things break easier and score more.
- **Anything you hold is a weapon.** Pick up a pan, a chair or a bottle and hit things with it. Breakable things break in your hand.
- **Wreck the room.** Tables and shelves give way after enough hits, lights pop and go dark, walls crack into holes.
- **Missions and achievements.** Short timed missions pop up during classic and zen rounds; 17 achievements are tracked across rounds.
- **Use the wreckage.** Legs, boards and beams from wrecked tables and shelves can be picked up and swung.
- **Open city.** Free roam a 4x4-block city with a park, gas station, parking lot, building site and waterfront. Day turns to night, showers roll through and the roads go shiny. Get into any parked car (E): sedans, taxis, vans and sports cars each handle differently, and R changes the radio station. Drive, ram things and wreck it. V switches between first person and a third-person view with a visible character; cars have a chase camera and a cockpit view. A mini-map shows where you are. Traffic drives the streets, waits at red lights and stops for whatever is in front of it; hit a car and it stops being traffic. In third person you play as an animated character (CC0 'Animated Human' by Quaternius) who walks, runs and swings.
- **More weapons.** A katana that slices things clean in two, a wrecking ball that swings down on a chain, a leaf blower, and a gravity gun that lifts anything (even a car) and flings it. Press G anywhere for a ground pound shockwave.
- **More rooms.** A museum (busts, vases and paintings behind glass, with an alarm), a supermarket (aisles, egg boxes, a freezer wall and trolleys to ram), Tiny town (you are a giant over a miniature city), a space station (low gravity, and broken windows suck everything out) and a bowling alley with real pins and strike scoring.
- **Even more rooms.** A restaurant kitchen in the dinner rush (new orders keep landing on the pass), a scrapyard with a hydraulic crusher that flattens cars and anything else on its bed, and a glass factory where bottles ride conveyor belts out of the furnace.
- **Chain reactions.** Gas cans set off the ones next to them, and some rooms start with a domino run snaking across the floor that ends at a gas can.
- **Break through walls.** Keep hitting the same spot and a wall gives way into a hidden room behind it, stocked with more things to smash.
- **Bosses.** Every so often a giant version of something in the room drops in. It takes a lot of hits, has a health bar, and is worth 300 points.
- **Phones and offline.** Touch controls with separate Grab, Throw (hold to throw harder) and Pound buttons; push the stick all the way to run. Install it to your home screen or desktop, and after the first visit it keeps working without a connection.
- **Zen garden.** No score and no clock: raked sand, soft rain and wind chimes, and everything breaks in slow motion and quietly.
- **Fire.** Explosions set wood alight. Flames spread to nearby furniture and crates, cook off gas cans, and burn things down; the fire extinguisher puts them out.
- **Toys.** A ball cannon that fires heavy iron balls, plus ramps (8) and trampolines (9) you can drop anywhere. Walk onto a trampoline and you bounce too.
- **Breakable floors.** Ground pound the same spot three times and you crash through into a basement full of old stuff. A ladder takes you back up.
- **Thunderstorms.** Tick Thunderstorm in the lobby: rain, lightning, real thunder, and now and then the power cuts out and you carry on by flashlight.
- **Pets.** Bring a cat or a dog (lobby). It wanders around, bolts under a table when things crash nearby, and purrs or wags when you pet it (E). Nothing can hurt it.
- **Things play together.** Cannon balls and the wrecking ball catch fire when they pass through flames and carry it on, a burning thing you throw lights whatever it lands in, and the leaf blower fans a fire along. Smoke alarms go off when a fire takes hold and the sprinklers come on and put it out. Car alarms set off the cars parked nearby.
- **Scrap cubes.** Whatever the scrapyard crusher flattens comes out as a scrap cube, stacked beside it, and a new car rolls in.
- **Draw dominoes.** In Build & smash press 1 and look along the floor to lay a domino line wherever you look; press 1 again to finish.
- **Silly physics.** A lobby switch for bouncy everything, big cartoon chunks and cartoon noises.
- **Zen garden extras.** Rake patterns in the sand with the broom, feed the koi (E by the pond), and swing at the wind chime to ring it.
- **Heavier hits.** The pause, shake and rumble on each hit now scale with how big the thing is and how hard you hit it.
- **Holidays.** Pumpkins at Halloween, baubles and gifts at Christmas, and fireworks over the outdoor scenes at New Year. Picked by date, or choose one in the lobby.
- **Scream to rage.** Optional: tick it in the lobby and shouting into your microphone fills the rage meter. The sound is only measured for loudness, never recorded or sent anywhere.
- **Smash your stress.** Type what's bothering you in the lobby; the words are written on plates, TVs and the clay figure for you to smash. They never leave the device.
- **Build & smash.** A no-timer mode: place domino runs, crate towers, glass pyramids, TV walls, bottle pins, plate towers and gas-can rows (keys 1 to 7), then knock them down.
- **New tools.** A paintball gun (colour splats everywhere, pops glass), a bowling ball and a fire extinguisher that blasts foam.
- **Clean up.** After a round, sweep the shards into a bin with a broom. Slow and satisfying.
- **Calm rooms.** A sunny greenhouse with birdsong and rain on the glass, and a beach shack at sunset with real wave recordings.
- **What a break leaves behind.** Spilled drinks spread into puddles, china leaves powder on the floor, dust hangs in the air and you hear the debris settle.
- **Downtown.** A city street at night: parked cars with alarms, shop windows, neon signs, a phone booth, a vending machine and fire hydrants that turn into geysers. Wrecking it builds Heat (up to five stars): a higher score multiplier, sirens closing in, and in a timed round five stars means you get Busted.
- **Bigger rooms.** Every room is larger, with more tables, shelves and things to break. The car yard has two cars.
- **Apartment.** A whole flat with a TV wall, fridge, glass coffee table, windows that crack before they fall out, and a bathroom mirror. Smash into the walls and you may hit a water pipe.
- **Unlocks from achievements.** Fire axe ("Bring the house down"), Golden bat ("Mission ace") and Blackout mode, any room in the dark with a flashlight ("Lights out").
- **Sledgehammer** knocks things into bigger chunks and punches wider holes in walls.
- **Settings and tutorial.** Mouse speed, invert look, field of view and screen shake in the lobby and pause menu. A short tutorial runs the first time you play.
- **Real breaking.** Objects split into pieces of themselves, smaller near the hit, keeping their shape and texture. Tougher things show damage first: cracks where you hit, broken flickering screens, smoke.
- **Damage that stays.** Cracks on the walls, dents in the car and on metal, liquid splashes, marks where thrown things hit.
- **3D sound.** Recorded breaks placed where they happen, an echo matched to each room, pieces tinkling as they land, footsteps, muffled ears after an explosion, and volume sliders.
- **Your photo.** Put a photo on cut-outs and picture frames, then smash them. The photo stays on your device and is never uploaded.
- **Unlockable weapons** (frying pan, golf club, pipe wrench) and local high scores, saved in your browser.

## Controls

| | Keyboard and mouse | Phone |
|---|---|---|
| Move / look | WASD + mouse | Left thumb / right thumb |
| Swing or punch | Click, or hold to wind up a heavy swing | Smash |
| Grab | E or right-click | Grab |
| Hit with what you hold | Click | Smash |
| Throw | E, or hold right-click for a power throw | Throw |
| Skip tutorial | T | Skip button |
| Drop | Q | |
| Bare hands | Click punch, F slap, Space kick | Smash, Kick |
| Shift | Run | |
| Pause | Esc | |

## Run it locally

It's plain HTML and JavaScript modules, no build step. Serve the folder with any static server:

```bash
python -m http.server 8790
```

Then open http://localhost:8790. (Opening `index.html` straight from disk won't work, browsers block modules there.)

## Tech

[three.js](https://threejs.org) for 3D and [cannon-es](https://github.com/pmndrs/cannon-es) for physics, both loaded from a CDN. Fallback sounds are generated in the browser.
